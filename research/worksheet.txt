OUR FIRST MONTH — paper prototype, fictional roleplay

Your task: work out an arrangement you could both follow for one month.
You can change the proposed budget. It is fine not to reach an agreement.

PROPOSED MONTHLY HOUSEHOLD COSTS
Rent                 $2,400
Utilities/internet     $250
Groceries              $600
Household supplies     $150
Total                $3,400

1. DEFINE WHAT IS SHARED
Which categories and amounts do you both accept? What stays personal?
What about restaurant meals, personal snacks or a new piece of furniture?
Do not add an amount twice if you move it between shared and personal costs.

2. COMPARE POSSIBLE ARRANGEMENTS
You can use equal amounts, a proportion of agreed take-home incomes, or
amounts you set yourselves. You may also change the household budget.
Personal commitments do not automatically change the chosen formula.
If you do not want to share incomes, you can discuss fixed contributions.
For each option, consider what remains for each person's other needs.
