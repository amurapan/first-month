PURPOSE
Observe whether individual people can understand the proposal, complete their
part, and explain the resulting plan. Two panel testers are not necessarily
partners. This study must not be described as a joint couple test.

PREFERRED AUDIENCE
US residents, 25–45, fluent English, desktop or laptop for the first build.
If a screener is available in the trial, ask neutrally:
Which describes your current living situation?
  — Living with a romantic partner for less than 12 months
  — Living with a romantic partner for 12 months or longer
  — Planning to move in with a romantic partner within three months
  — Living separately with no move planned / other
For discovery, prefer the first option. If this narrow audience is unavailable
before the deadline, use broader adult testers for usability and label the
sample accordingly. Do not silently count them as target-customer validation.

STUDY TITLE
Planning shared household expenses

INTRODUCTION
You are testing an early prototype, not your finances or relationship. Please
think aloud. Use the fictional details supplied by the demo. Do not enter
your own financial information. It is fine to disagree with a proposed plan.
The partner shown in the single-person demo is simulated and clearly labelled.

TASKS — target 10–15 minutes; adapt to the actual deployed build
1. Before starting, explain what you think this page helps you do. What
   outcome would you expect? Do not read our explanation to the tester.
2. Using the fictional profile supplied by the demo, enter your constraints
   and decide what you would share. Explain what you expect the partner to see.
3. Compare the available arrangements. Choose one you can explain or mark
   what prevents you from choosing. There is no preferred split method.
4. Review the plan. Explain how much each person contributes, what expenses
   are included, who pays the bills, and when money changes hands.
5. The demo changes one income. Update the proposal. Explain whether the
   old agreement is still in effect and what the other person needs to do.
6. Which part, if any, would be useful compared with how you currently handle
   shared expenses? Which part would you skip? A preference for your existing
   method is useful feedback too.

OBSERVE
Misunderstood sharing, incorrect explanation of amounts, incomplete payment
instructions, confusion about the other person's agreement, unnecessary steps.
Keep past behavior, observed actions and opinions separate in notes.

RECORD HONESTLY
Two individual usability sessions, if both complete. Not two couple interviews,
not proof of willingness to pay, not observed retention. Actual target-audience
eligibility and completion times are unknown until the sessions take place.

DO NOT LAUNCH UNTIL
The demo works end to end, simulated partner is labelled, there are no real
financial data in the demo, the free allowance is visible, and ordering the
test does not require a paid plan. Do not start a paid subscription.
