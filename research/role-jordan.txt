ROLE B — JORDAN
Give this page only to the person playing Jordan. All details are fictional.

You and Alex are preparing to live together. Your monthly take-home
income is $3,000. You have $300 in personal commitments each month.
After those commitments and your contribution to shared household costs,
you want at least $1,200 available for your remaining spending and saving.
These amounts are invented for this exercise, not suggested financial rules.

You want a routine you can manage without repeatedly asking Alex to cover
shortfalls. You want to understand what happens if your income changes.
You have not already decided that any particular split is best.

Before talking with Alex, write:
1. What matters most to you in the arrangement?
2. What monthly contribution would you initially propose, and why?
3. Which details from this card are you comfortable sharing?
4. What do you need to ask Alex before deciding?

Then use worksheet.txt together. Reveal only what you choose to share.
You may propose changes, ask for time, or leave a disagreement unresolved.
Do not use your own real income or account details in this roleplay.
