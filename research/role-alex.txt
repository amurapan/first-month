ROLE A — ALEX
Give this page only to the person playing Alex. All details are fictional.

You and Jordan are preparing to live together. Your monthly take-home
income is $6,000. You have $900 in personal commitments each month.
After those commitments and your contribution to shared household costs,
you want at least $2,000 available for your remaining spending and saving.
These amounts are invented for this exercise, not suggested financial rules.

You want a predictable routine and do not want to track every small purchase.
You would prefer to keep your existing bank account. You can discuss other
approaches. You have not already decided that any particular split is best.

Before talking with Jordan, write:
1. What matters most to you in the arrangement?
2. What monthly contribution would you initially propose, and why?
3. Which details from this card are you comfortable sharing?
4. What do you need to ask Jordan before deciding?

Then use worksheet.txt together. Reveal only what you choose to share.
You may propose changes, ask for time, or leave a disagreement unresolved.
Do not use your own real income or account details in this roleplay.
